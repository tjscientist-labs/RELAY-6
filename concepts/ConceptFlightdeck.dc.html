CONCEPT B

FLIGHT​DECK

A 1960s blockhouse console in an orange field case. Six red missile-switch covers, one per pad, then a hazard-striped fire zone you work with both hands.
Missile switches — lift a cover and flip the toggle to put that pad in the shot, then read the status bar above it. Every lifted cover is in the salvo, so the panel shows what will fire.
Two-hand fire — ARM on the left, FIRE on the right, 450 mm apart, both held. One hand can't fire it by accident.
Lid — an OLED in lime phosphor, next to a backlit checklist card you can swap for your club's procedure.
Master key + e-stop — both in the fire zone, where your hands already are.
Case: safety-orange 1500-size shell. Room for a big battery.
Panel: 3 mm aluminium, powder-coated, with engraved legends. Hazard stripe in vinyl.
Feels like: a Gemini blockhouse in a box.
