CONCEPT C

MONO​LITH

Matte black, cold white light, and nothing on the panel you don't need. Six brushed throttle levers set the shot, and a twist-collar core fires it, like a starship helm.
Throttle levers — push a lever forward to commit its pad. Hall sensors read where it sits, and a light slot glows under each lever that is in.
Firing order — six light columns in the lid show the ripple sequence and each pad's turn, so a 1.5 s ripple reads at a glance.
Core — twist the knurled collar 90° to arm. It springs back to safe the moment you let go. Press the lit core to fire.
Twin OLED strip — two 3.12″ panels butted into a 512 × 64 strip, wide enough for the whole countdown.
Case: 1400-size shell in black, the edges wrapped in carbon vinyl.
Panel: CNC-milled 6 mm Delrin. Lever slots are routed, with light-pipes under them.
Feels like: 2001 meets a shuttle throttle quadrant.
