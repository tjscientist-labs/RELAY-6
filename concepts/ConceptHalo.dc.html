CONCEPT A

HALO

Six pad keys ring a covered FIRE button inside an edge-lit acrylic halo. The ring is the countdown: it drains in orange, segment by segment, to zero.
Pad keys — each key lights its pad's state: green means continuity, orange means selected, red means open igniter, dark means empty. Press a key to select; hold MODE for salvo.
FIRE — a 60 mm button under a clear flip cover. It does nothing until the key is turned and the pad box ACKs.
Lid — a 3.12″ OLED in a machined bezel over hex-mesh, with a speaker grille and a fold-down antenna.
Left column — arm key, an NFC pad for the RSO card, and banana jacks for the wired backup.
Right column — mode encoder, plus a full-size e-stop that cuts pyro power in hardware.
Case: Pelican 1300-size shell (Apache 2800 or similar). Gunmetal, with orange latches.
Panel: 3 mm black acrylic, laser-cut. Clear 6 mm ring, edge-lit by 24 WS2812s.
Feels like: a reactor-core starter switch.
