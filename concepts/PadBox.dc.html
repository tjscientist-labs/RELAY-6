PAD BOX · SHARED BY ALL THREE CONCEPTS

RELAY-6

Six outputs, one box, out at the rack. It does the safety thinking, not the controller: it fires only when its own key is in ARM, the controller's packet checks out, and the link heartbeat is less than a second old.
Per-pad channel
Logic-level MOSFET, 30 A+ pulse, PowerPole out. Like TeleFire's FET outputs.
Continuity + Ω
Sense current kept at about 100 µA, TeleLaunch's figure. The ADC reads the resistance, not just yes or no.
Hardware arm
The key switch drives a contactor on the pyro bus. In SAFE, igniter power is physically gone.
Watchdog
No valid heartbeat for 1 s drops the contactor. A packet from a stranger never gets past the AES check.
Clip leads
Whip leads made from extension cord and alligator clips, as in the TeleLaunch manual's appendix.
Shell
A .50-cal ammo can, as TeleFire uses, or a small matching case. The mast stows inside.
Six pads fit one box. For more, the protocol carries a bank number the way TeleLaunch does, so a second RELAY-6 can join later without a redesign.
