MODEL ROCKETRY · LAUNCHERS · DESIGN STUDY 01

Six-pad wireless launch controller

Two-way radio · OLED · fire one pad or all six · a sci-fi case built on a Pelican-style shell
REV 0.1 · 2026-11-18
CONCEPT · NOT A BUILD SPEC
01 · BRIEF
A hand-carried controller at the flight line talks to a pad box out at the rack. The radio link goes both ways: the box reports back on every pad before anything can fire. Up to six rockets: fire one, pick several, or salvo all six. The case should look like it belongs on a starship, and it has to survive a field.
02 · SOURCES
TeleLaunch (Altus Metrum): 2-way 70 cm radio, AES-authenticated packets, 100 µA continuity check, local and remote arm, hold-to-launch, drag races, Pelican 1170 controller.
Overkill Launch Controller (Edward Robinson, 2020): briefcase panel in carbon wrap, security code, 15 s countdown, big e-stop, mode switches, wired backup, beacon light.
Video walkthrough of the same controller (YouTube, 14 Apr 2020).
03 · SAFETY, NON-NEGOTIABLE
• Removable key in series with the fire circuit, at both ends
• Fire control springs back to OFF when released
• No arm without an ACK from the pad box
• Link lost for more than 1 s → the pad box disarms itself
• E-stop removes pyro power in hardware, not just in code
• Wired backup for the day the radio won't play

05 · NEW IDEAS, BEYOND THE SOURCES
Five fire modes
Single · Select (any set) · Salvo (all six) · Ripple (staggered, 0.25–5 s) · Drag race (selected, together)
Igniter ohmmeter
Each pad reports igniter resistance, not just yes/no: an open, a short, or a clip that's half on.
Ignition timer
The pad box sees the igniter burn open and reports "P3 lit in 184 ms". It also catches a no-fire straight away.
Wind go/no-go
A cup anemometer on the pad box; gusts over 20 mph lock out FIRE and say why.
Talking countdown
Speaker calls "Pad three, armed … five, four …"; the Overkill controller left a speaker slot for this.
RSO tag arming
Key switch plus an NFC card. Only a tagged card can arm, and the log records who armed.
Flight log
Every shot logged: time, pad, motor, Ω, wind, GPS. To microSD, or as a QR code on the OLED.
Pad beacon
RGB mast light: green safe · amber armed · strobing red counting. Readable from the line at 50 m.
