All three, one pad box, open to build

HALO, FLIGHTDECK and MONOLITH share one electronics core and one RELAY-6 pad box, and any of them pairs with it. The radio is licence-free LoRa, so anyone can build one, and every packet is checked and signed. The pad box is rated for high-power rockets and clusters.

CRC catches a corrupted packet. CMAC catches one the system didn't send: a stray remote, another launcher, noise. A CRC alone can't do that, because anyone can compute one.
Counter + a challenge nonce for ARM mean a recorded FIRE packet can't be replayed. Pairing over USB or an NFC tap writes a new random key, and there is no default key.
Heartbeat runs at 4 Hz both ways. After 1 s without a valid one, the pad box drops the pyro contactor. This is the fix for a garage-door-opener launcher that fires on its own.
