Proposed GPIO map, controller (Heltec WiFi LoRa 32 V3)

Pins used on the board itself and left alone: LoRa SX1262 on GPIO 8–14, the on-board 0.96″ OLED on I²C GPIO 17/18 with reset on 21, Vext switch 36, LED 35, PRG button 0, USB-serial on 43/44, battery sense 1 with 37. Check every row against Heltec's current V3 pinout before soldering (Stage 2); GPIO 0, 3, 45 and 46 are ESP32-S3 strapping pins and are avoided for inputs that could be held at boot.
